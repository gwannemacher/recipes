# Taro Abacus Beads with Mushroom Pork Sauce

This recipe is taken from the book The Hakka Cookbook by Linda Lou Anusasananan

## Ingredients

### Sauce
- [ ] 1 1/2 cups chicken broth
- [ ] 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- [ ] 1 tablespoon dark soy sauce
- [ ] 4 teaspoons cornstarch
- [ ] 1 1/2 teaspoons sugar
- [ ] 1/4 teaspoon salt

### Stir-fry
- [ ] 16 dried or fresh shiitake mushrooms, each about 2 to 3 inches wide
- [ ] 6 green onions
- [ ] Taro abacus beads
- [ ] 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- [ ] 1 tablespoon minced garlic
- [ ] 8 ounces ground pork

## Directions

### 1. For the sauce:
In a small bowl, mix the broth, soy sauce, dark soy sauce, cornstarch, sugar, and salt.

### 2. For the stir-fry:
Rinse the mushrooms. If using dried mushrooms, place in a small bowl and cover with hot water. Soak until soft. Drain and squeeze excess water out of the soaked mushrooms. Remove and discard the stems from the dried or fresh mushrooms. Cut the caps into 1/4-inch wide strips. Cut the green onions into 3-inch lengths, and then cut lengthwise into 1/4-inch-thick slivers.

### 3.
Boil the taro abacus beads or place made-ahead cooked abacus beads in very hot water as directed in the recipe.

### 4.
Set a wok over high heat. When the pan is hot, after about 1 minute, add the oil and rotate the pan to spread. Add the garlic and pork; stir-fry until the pork is browned and crumbly, about 2 minutes. Stir in the mushrooms and stir-fry until lightly browned, 1 to 2 minutes.

### 5.
Drain the taro abacus beads. Add the abacus beads and green onions to the pan. Cook and turn the abacus beads gently until lightly browned on both sides, 1 to 2 minutes. Stir the sauce mixture and add it to the pan. Stir gently until the sauce thickens and boils, about 1 minute. Spoon the abacus beads onto plates or a serving dish.

### Note:
If preparing up to 2 days ahead, when the abacus beads are cool, drain them well in a colander, and then pour onto oiled plates or pans and spread the pieces slightly apart in a single layer. Cover and chill. When ready to use, immerse the abacus beads in a bowl of very hot water and let stand about 5 minutes. Drain just before stir-frying.
