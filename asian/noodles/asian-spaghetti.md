# Asian Spaghetti

This recipe is taken from the book Chinese Enough by Kristina Cho (under the name Creamy Tomato Udon).

## Ingredients

- [ ] 2 lbs frozen udon noodles (4 bundles)
- [ ] 4 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 1 yellow onion, diced
- [ ] 1 (1-inch) piece fresh ginger, peeled and minced
- [ ] 1/4 cup tomato paste
- [ ] 1 tsp kosher salt
- [ ] 1 tsp sugar
- [ ] 1/2 tsp ground white paper
- [ ] 1/2 teaspoon curry powder
- [ ] 1/2 tsp crushed red chili flakes
- [ ] 3/4 cup full-fat canned coconut milk
- [ ] 2 Tbsp fish sauce
- [ ] Thinly sliced green onions
- [ ] Fried garlic

## Directions

- [ ] Boil large pot water. Add noodles - blanch until loose and bouncy, 45-60 seconds. 
- [ ] Reserve 1/2 cup noodle water for the sauce, then drain and rinse under cold water
- [ ] In large skillet, heat oil over medim heat. Add garlic, onion, and ginger and cook, stirring occasionally, until tender and starting to brown around the edges, 5-6 minutes
- [ ] Add tomato paste, salt, sugar, white pepper, curry powder, and chili flakes
- [ ] Stir to combine and cook for 1 minute to reduce the paste
- [ ] Add reserved noodle water, coconut milk, and fish sauce
- [ ] Stir until smooth and combined and bring to a simmer
- [ ] Add udon and toss to evenly coat in the sauce
- [ ] Transfer noodles to platter and top with green onions and fried garlic
