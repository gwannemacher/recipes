# Dried Shrimp and Spring Onion Oil

This recipe is taken from the book Every Grain of Rice by Fuchsia Dunlop.

## Ingredients

- [ ] 2 Tbsp dried shrimp
- [ ] 2 tsp Shaoxing wine
- [ ] 4 spring onions
- [ ] 2 tsp light soy sauce
- [ ] 6 Tbsp cooking oil

## Directions

- [ ] Place the dried shrimp in a small bowl with the Shaoxing wine and just enough hot water to cover them. Set aside for 30 minutes
- [ ] Smack the spring onions lightly with the side of a cleaver to open up the white parts slightly, then cut them evenly into 2 1/2-3in sections
- [ ] Heat the oil in a woke over a high flame. Add the spring onions and stir-fry until they are turning a little golden
- [ ] Drain the shrimp, add them to the wok, and continue to stir-fry until the spring onions are well-browned and wonderfully fragrant, but not burned
- [ ] Mix in with soy sauce
- [ ] I like to use this oil on top of noodles with some grated cucumber
