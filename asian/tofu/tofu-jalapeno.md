# Tofu with Jalapenos

## Ingredients

### Marinating

- [ ] 4-5 jalapenos, sliced
- [ ] 2 teaspoons Shaoxing wine
- [ ] Small dollop soy sauce
- [ ] 1 Tablespoon black bean sauce
- [ ] 1 teaspoon cornstarch
- [ ] 4-5 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 2 green onion, sliced

### Braising

- [ ] 1 block soft tofu, cut into bite-sized pieces
- [ ] 1 cup chicken broth
- [ ] 1 teaspoon chicken MSG powder
- [ ] Small dollop oyster sauce

### After

- [ ] 1 teaspoon cornstarch in water
- [ ] 2 teaspoons fermented black beans

## Directions

- [ ] Cook jalapeno mixture for a minute
- [ ] Add braising ingredients
- [ ] Once hot add cornstarch slurry, then stir in fermented black beans
