# Bamboo shoot and mushroom-stuffed Yuba Rolls

This recipe is the book Chinese Homestyle, by Maggie Zhu

## Ingredients

- [ ] 6 dried shiitake mushrooms
- [ ] 1 cup hot water
- [ ] 3 tablespoons peanut oil, divided
- [ ] 1 teaspoon minced ginger
- [ ] 1 scallion, thinly sliced
- [ ] 1/2 cup gulienned carrots
- [ ] 1/2 cup hulienned bamboo shoots
- [ ] 2 teaspoons toasted sesame oil
- [ ] 2 tablespoons light soy sauce
- [ ] 2 teaspoons dark soy sauce
- [ ] 1 tablespoon sugar
- [ ] 16 fresh yuba sheets

## Directions

- [ ] In a small bowl, cover the dried shiitake mushrooms with the hot water and soak for 20 minutes, or until tender throughout. Squeeze out excess water from the mushrooms and rub off any debris. Reserve the soaking water. Remove and discard the tough stems and slice the mushroom caps into thin pieces.
- [ ] In a medium skillet, heat 2 tablespoons of the peanut oil over medium-high heat until hot. Add the ginger and scallion, stirring a few times to release the fragrance. Add the mushrooms and cook and stir for 1 minute. Add the carrots and bamboo shoots and cook and stir for 1 minute. Transfer the contents of the pan to a plate to completely cool.
- [ ] In a small bowl, combine 1/3 cup of the reserved mushroom soaking water (avoiding adding any debris in the water), the sesame oil, light and dark soy sauces, and sugar for the sauce and stir to mix well.
- [ ] Layer 2 yuba sheets vertically on a clean work surface. Spread about 2 tablespoons of filling in the center, about 1 inch from the bottom and leaving about 1 inch on each side. Fold up the bottom over the filling, then fold the left and right sides inward to cover the filling entirely. Fold up the filling to the top to form a roll. Place it seam side down on a large plate or tray. Repeat for the remaining yuba sheets and filling.
- [ ] In a medium skillet, heat the remaining 1 tablespoon peanut oil over medium heat until warm. Add the yuba rolls, seam sides down, without overlapping. Reduce the heat to medium-low or low. Cook, flipping frequently, until both sides turn golden brown.
- [ ] Pour in the sauce and stir gently a few times. Simmer until the sauce is reduced and slightly thickened, 1 minute or so. Transfer the contents of the pan to a large serving plate. Serve hot or cold.
- [ ] To store yuba rolls, place in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days or in the freezer for up to 3 months. Reheat in the microwave in 1-minute intervals until heated through. You don't need to thaw frozen yuba rolls before reheating.
