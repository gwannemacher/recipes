# Traditional Taiwanese Sticky Rice

This recipe is taken from the book Tiffy Cooks by Tiffy Chen.

## Ingredients

- [ ] 2 cups white glutinous rice
- [ ] 1 cup dried shiitake mushrooms
- [ ] 1 tablespoon oil
- [ ] 4 shallots, sliced
- [ ] 1/4 cup sliced peeled fresh ginger
- [ ] 1/2 cup chopped peeled carrot
- [ ] 1/2 cup raw cashews
- [ ] 3 tablespoons light soy sauce
- [ ] 3 tablespoons oyster sauce
- [ ] 3 tablespoons sesame oil
- [ ] 6 lotus leaves (optional)

## For garnish

- [ ] Fried shallots
- [ ] Fresh cilantro leaves

## Directions

- [ ] Place the glutinous rice in a large bowl and cover with 8 cups of water. Let soak at room temperature for 4 hours or in the fridge, covered, for 8 hours or overnight. Drain and set aside.
- [ ] Place the dried shiitake mushrooms in a medium bowl and cover with 1 2/3 cups of water. Let soak for 30 minutes or until softened. Strain the mushrooms, reserving the soaking liquid. Squeeze the mushrooms dry and cut into thin strips.
- [ ] Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Add the shallots and ginger and cook, stirring constantly, for 1 to 2 minutes, until fragrant.
- [ ] Add the carrots, cashews, and mushrooms. Increase the heat to medium-high and saute for 1 to 2 minutes, until the mushrooms start to sweat. Stir in the light soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sesame oil. Add the glutinous rice and mix well to ensure all the rice is coated with the sauce.
- [ ] Pour in the reserved mushroom soaking liquid and cook, gently folding the rice until it has soaked up all the sauce, 3 to 5 minutes. Be gentle to prevent breaking the rice.
- [ ] Fill a pot that will fit a bamboo steamer about a third of the way with water. Bring to boil over high heat.
- [ ] Line the steamer with perforated parchment paper or lotus leaves. Pour the rice into the steamer, and using a chopstick, poke holes in the rice to allow the steam to rise up evenly. Make as many holes as you can to ensure the rice cooks evenly.
- [ ] Place the steamer over the boiling water, cover, and steam the rice for 25 minutes. Remove the steamer from the pot and let the rice sit, covered, for 15 minutes. Transfer the rice to a large serving bowl or individual bowls. Garnish with the fried shallots and cilantro. Serve immediately.
